# Persisted route and actual geometry correspondence

The route checker now binds the displayed route to the complete materialization artifact it independently checks. A standalone candidate must contain exactly one route and exactly that changed route identity, plus the one authorized host identity when applicable. Duplicate route, changed-component, demand, terminal and physical-part identities are rejected. The joint checker retains its complete multi-route inventory and prior-route obligations, and now rejects duplicate aggregate changed IDs.

The route ID, explicit point sequence, geometry artifact root, service, section, immutable scenario root, source/frame and export instruction must agree. The standalone source/path/root pointer must identify the same checked artifact. Composite materializations may replace the file and artifact root while retaining prior route definitions; exported copies and relocated paths are resolved through the existing store mechanism.

Exact metadata binding is supplemented by a separate numerical geometric check. It reopens each actual IFC straight or circular elbow, checks the ordered GUID/STEP/component denominator, directed line membership, circle radius, angle, tangent frame, connected endpoints and actual route/size provenance. These checks use every declared polyline leg and do not call the route exporter or treat its expected-geometry fields as the geometric oracle. The correspondence tolerance is 1e-7 m, with dimensionless angular/frame comparisons stated at the same numerical threshold. Full native volume, port-cap attachment, clearance, source preservation and zone checks remain separate.

IFC2X3 uses its existing generic flow segment/fitting entities with the declared route service provenance; IFC4 uses the declared pipe/duct classes. A composite route's old SourceSHA256 property may describe an intermediate append input. It is not treated as authority for the final source binding: the immutable source bytes and complete final materialization are checked directly.

Optional nominal fabrication evidence is checked through `check_route_fabrication_evidence`, using the candidate's immutable run and baseline. The checker invokes it even when the reference is absent. Historical absence may be NOT_APPLICABLE; removal of required published or preserved evidence fails. A valid certificate proves its stated PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN nominal disposition, with no transfer of native acceptance or automatic pruning authority. Evidence mappings may not name phantom routes.
