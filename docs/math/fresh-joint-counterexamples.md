# Fresh native counterexamples for simultaneous routes

This backend optimization rejects a current materialized assignment after a
fresh failure of one required cross-route predicate. A passing assignment still
requires every existing physical, semantic, engineering and objective check.
The native result uses the existing numerical CAD contract; it is not an exact
real-solid theorem or a continuous routing infeasibility certificate.

For an assignment A, feasibility implies every required pair predicate P(A,i,j).
An applicable current failure of one such predicate therefore refutes A without
evaluating the remaining conjuncts. An earlier assignment's failure does not
establish P for A. Its participant locations are only hints about which current
pair to inspect first.

## Integration and authority

`routing/joint.py` retains up to sixteen failed-pair locations from the same
frozen generated menu. A location binds a demand's choice definition and part
index/kind, or a protected prior route definition. Each visited assignment is
still materialized, with fresh route GUIDs and a complete replacement IFC. The
Cartesian traversal, candidate budget and final native objective selection are
unchanged. No pair cut skips a future assignment or claims universal physical
incompatibility across different realizations.

`routing/checker.py` can pause its own invocation after current input, mission,
materialization and IFC semantic checks. The pause is a private one-use generator
continuation, not a serialized certificate or caller-authored preflight. The
joint checker prepares every route and checks composite mission coverage before
allowing a hint to reach the native child. It closes all continuations on every
exit. Duplicate ownership, changed definitions, unauthorized effects and
incomplete mappings cannot authorize an early pair verdict.

`routing/joint_negative_probe.py` resolves the hint to the current parts and runs
at most sixteen pairs in a supervised frozen child with a ten-second local
deadline. It independently reconstructs current source frames, reopens actual
complete native bodies, validates GUID/STEP membership, and applies the greater
of the two current clearance requirements with the existing 1 micrometre
numerical tolerance. Independent routes receive no shared-junction contact
exemption. Only a fresh `POSITIVE_COMMON_SOLID_VOLUME` or `CLEARANCE_VIOLATION`
failure can reject the current assignment early.

PASS, UNKNOWN, missing native support, failed mapping, child errors, local
timeout and abnormal process exit provide no counterexample. They resume the
complete checker when current inputs remain intact. Cancellation propagates.
An interrupted child cannot contribute a partially written FAIL.

## Input and prior-state preservation

The child hashes the exact stream copied into exclusive per-invocation input
files before parsing. Both replacement geometry and federation reconstruction
use these private copies. Rehashing a public pathname before and after parsing
alone is insufficient: an A-to-B-to-A replacement can make both hashes observe A
while the parser retains B. The independently reproduced real-geometry failure
and its corrected test remain in `evidence/release/joint-probe-native-audit/`.
Private input files are owned by the local checker invocation; this is not a
claim of isolation from a hostile process with arbitrary access to checker
memory or its private files.

Current original sources, replacement files and prior accepted export files are
also rehashed around the optional probe and before publication. A changed input
produces an integrity failure with no retained pair verdict or objective.

The preflight now protects the inverse effects of appended relationships against
the entire prior accepted materialization, including its route parts, ports and
systems. Equality of original STEP strings alone misses a newly appended
property/group/nesting relationship. Explicit new-part spatial containment and
separately checked terminal connections retain their existing scope. Tests
reproduce the formerly passing property attack and reject both unauthorized
prior-to-prior port connections and branches onto prior ports.

## Evidence and validation scope

An early report contains `native-cross-route-counterexample: FAIL` and a rooted
fresh native artifact. Its objective is empty. Source scans, remaining physical
obligations, the complete cross-route denominator and joint objective remain
NOT_RUN. The probe expressly carries no feasibility, acceptance or objective
authority. A report's early numerical counterexample does not establish a whole
building's engineering compliance or a global optimum.

`tests/test_joint_negative_integration.py` exercises actual three-demand,
four-assignment materialization, new GUID remapping, one freshly recomputed
crossing, fully checked separated alternatives and fabricated old hints.
`tests/test_joint_negative_hint_adversarial.py` covers current requirements,
frames, source mutation, missing/duplicate membership, ambiguous/native failures,
timeouts, cancellation and protected inverse effects.
`tests/test_joint_probe_native_audit.py` independently checks real transient
geometry substitution and unrelated native body redirection.

The real Office comparison lives under
`evidence/benchmarks/joint-counterexample/office/`. Its original two-demand
mission remains unchanged; a separately frozen mission explicitly adds one
parallel service with two alternatives. Baseline and optimized attempts must
match that specification root. Work counts distinguish performed source pairs
from early NOT_RUN obligations, and phase timings include their stated report
and selection overhead. Export acceptance is established only by the separate
fresh exported-byte report, never inferred from a passing proposal or hint.
