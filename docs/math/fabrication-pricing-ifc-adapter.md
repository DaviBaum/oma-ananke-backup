# Source-bound fabrication pricing in the route workflow

`routing/certified_fabrication.py` now invokes the exact finite-graph pricing kernel after retaining an independently checked feasible graph path. `routing/proposals.py` publishes the model, coverage, objective, certificates and separate proof checks as an immutable search artifact. Ordinary and simultaneous route workflows consume these proposals through their existing fabrication, native, service and acceptance checks.

The complete source ledger is still freshly checked against the current files, source frames, mission and implementation. Full-body obstacle omissions are independently replayed; translating-ball omissions are not inherited. Every emitted binary64 path receives its own fixed-polyline fabrication proof against the complete conservative outer model. Source hashes and executable identity are compared again before publication.

The nominal pricing objective uses the active scenario's `length_m` and `fitting_count` weights. Missing dimensions have zero weight. Weights use the exact declared decimal values, matching the candidate-selection scalarization policy. The graph geometry retains its exact binary-input interpretation. The nominal length includes trimmed straights and circular quarter arcs, so a cost is an exact rational pair denoting `a + b*pi`. Native measured length is a separate numerical quantity; neither a native cost lower bound nor continuous optimality follows.

A successfully checked optimum is proposed first. If its converted path differs from the previously checked feasible path, that fallback remains as a separate alternative. Exact duplicate paths are emitted once. Each proposal binds the particular `FABRICATION_PRICING` or `FABRICATION_SEARCH` certificate and its own binary64 fabrication certificate; a priced path is never attributed to a different breadth-first path witness. The artifact's nominal optimum assertion concerns the exact rational path in the declared finite graph. It is not an optimum assertion for the converted/native candidate.

The optional producer, independent pricing replay and converted-path check share a local time allowance of at most six seconds and 60% of the remaining adapter deadline. Work and pi-comparison precision limits also apply. Inconclusive or rejected pricing evidence leaves the already checked feasible route available. Expiry of the overall adapter deadline, changed source bytes, changed executable or caller cancellation prevents publication. An exception raised by the caller retains its original identity and cannot be mistaken for the adapter's optional pricing timeout.

Search artifacts are diagnostic evidence under the local producer/store trust boundary. Their hashes bind content; arbitrary self-authored artifacts do not gain source authenticity from hashing. They do not replace the independently checked physical candidate report or confer acceptance authority. Complete native solid checks, nonadjacent self-interference, service requirements, actual IFC directrix correspondence, immutable source preservation and a fresh exported-byte check remain separate obligations.

The initial `ebe15db2...` adapter checkpoint passes 62 tests, including a real IFC source with two different length/fitting policies. Length-only chooses four elbows with exact nominal cost `4+pi`; a fitting penalty of one chooses one elbow with cost `8+pi/4`. Both actual fabricated IFC results pass independent native checks. A separate 75-test pricing/audit checkpoint includes seven adapter attacks: valid certificates from another objective or source context, caller cancellation, and source/executable changes before publication. These are overlapping scoped executions, not additive unique-test counts. Evidence is retained under `evidence/release/fabrication-pricing-*.xml`.

The same frozen build passes 21 ordinary/joint workflow tests in 98.41 seconds, including native candidate checks, acceptance, actual exported IFC rechecks, proposal identity and the adapter's cost/fallback boundaries. This is a scoped backend checkpoint, not a complete production release.
