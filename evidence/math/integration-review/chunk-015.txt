[16953] Case C — Candidate routing infeasibility
[16954] Every exact candidate materialization has a complete PCROUTE infeasibility proof.
[16955] Case D — Mixed branch proof
[16956] An exhaustive outer branch tree closes every leaf by master, materialization, routing, or joint-factor infeasibility.
[16957] Under the applicable complete case hypotheses,
[16958] \[ \eta\in\mathcal I_{\mathrm{JCD}}. \]
[16960] Each case rules out every represented joint witness at a different layer. In Case D, exhaustive disjoint branches cover the master domain and every leaf contains no joint witness. Therefore the joint fiber is empty. ∎
[16962] 86. What the whole theorems do not prove
[16963] They do not prove:
[16964] that the baseline captures every real fact;
[16965] that every legal architectural rewrite is represented;
[16966] that every physical route class is represented;
[16967] that every objective has been included;
[16968] that every authority accepts the formalism;
[16969] that a bounded-gap result is exact;
[16970] that a scalar optimum is the full Pareto frontier;
[16971] that a complete represented Pareto set is the universal real-world Pareto set;
[16972] that any dual is an architecture derivative;
[16973] that every conflict can be lifted;
[16974] that future theory changes preserve the package;
[16975] that a current release remains valid forever.
[16977] 87. Open-world theorem
[16978] THM-JCD101 — Open-world co-design limitation
[16979] Construct worlds \(W_0,W_1\) with identical:
[16981] master schema;
[16982] rewrite transactions;
[16983] candidate PCSIR;
[16985] cuts;
[16987] objectives;
[16988] PCJCD.
[16989] In \(W_1\), add an unmodeled structural restriction prohibiting one accepted opening. Let the selected design remain acceptable in \(W_0\) and become unacceptable in \(W_1\).
[16990] The formal checker sees identical artifacts and produces the same verdict. Therefore it cannot prove absence of the unmodeled restriction.
[16992] \[ \boxed{ \textbf{Even exact PCJCD optimality is optimality only inside the represented design space, geometry, topology, routing model, physics, constraints, objectives, authority, evidence, versions, and checkers.} } \]
[16995] 88. Proposal-source independence
[16996] THM-JCD102 — Final JCD proposal-source independence
[16997] A complete JCD candidate, cut proposal, rewrite proposal, or search order may originate from:
[16998] AI;
[16999] human designer;
[17000] MIP/CP solver;
[17001] heuristic;
[17002] random search;
[17003] rule system.
[17004] If acceptance depends only on checked typed artifacts, replacing the proposer while preserving those artifacts leaves every formal conclusion unchanged.
[17006] Proposer identity is absent from the semantic premises except as provenance. The same normalized artifacts invoke the same deterministic checkers and receive the same verdicts. ∎
