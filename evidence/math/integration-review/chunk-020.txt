[20359] \(H_1\) concentrates more load in high-factor hours and yields \(540\) synthetic carbon units. \(H_2\) shifts flexible loads and yields \(470\). \(H_2\) has \(25\) more initial embodied units.

[20362] These are synthetic modeled values, not empirical hotel results.

[20365] \[ \begin{array}{c|cc} &\mathbb E[Z]&\operatorname{CVaR}_{0.95}(Z)\\\hline H_1&100&240\\ H_2&108&160 \end{array} \]
[20366] Expectation prefers \(H_1\); tail risk prefers \(H_2\). Neither is universally preferable without an authorized policy.
