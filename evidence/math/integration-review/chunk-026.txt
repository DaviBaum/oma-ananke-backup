[24404] THM-CMP127 — Whole compiler reproducibility theorem
[24405] Under byte-deterministic producers, captured versions/environment/seeds, and deterministic canonicalization, equal build inputs produce byte-identical release artifacts. If producers differ but validators are deterministic, only verdict reproducibility follows. ∎
[24406] THM-CMP128 — Whole compiler branch/transaction theorem

[24408] THM-CMP129 — Whole compiler release-lattice theorem

[24412] THM-CMP131 — Whole compiler feasible-building theorem

[24426] THM-CMP137 — Whole compiler crash-recovery theorem
