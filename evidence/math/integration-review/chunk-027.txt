[25310] Let the baseline root contain AHU \(A\) in room \(R\).

[25312] \[ \text{move AHU }A \text{ from }(1,1,0)\text{ to }(2,1,0). \]
[25313] Transaction \(\tau_B\):
[25314] \[ \text{rename unrelated room }Q \text{ from “Storage” to “Archive.”} \]

[25317] the display label is not used by any engineering rule;
[25318] authority roots are independent;
