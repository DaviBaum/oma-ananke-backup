[3257] CONJ-SIR1 — Sparse coupled state. Most building co-design problems share a moderate cross-component identity boundary rather than requiring every internal object in every solver.
Falsification: nearly every geometric detail changes every systems computation.
[3258] CONJ-SIR2 — Exact semantic pullback is practical. The identity-consistency pullback can be checked cheaply relative to geometry and routing synthesis.
Falsification: cross-store identity reconciliation dominates end-to-end runtime.
[3259] CONJ-SIR3 — Decision-sufficient route envelopes are compact. A small family of envelope templates captures most foundational routing semantics.
Falsification: almost every route requires bespoke continuous body models.
[3260] CONJ-SIR4 — Separator state is sparse. Floor, shaft, and corridor partitions admit small exact interfaces.
Falsification: route and constraint coupling makes separators nearly as large as whole floors.
[3261] CONJ-SIR5 — Contextual router quotients materially compress state. ANANKE needs much less router state than the router internally maintains.
Falsification: most internal route distinctions change a downstream decision.
[3262] CONJ-SIR6 — Exact/bounded hybrid geometry is sufficient. Exact combinatorial predicates plus sound outer enclosures can certify useful building routes.
Falsification: high-impact decisions routinely depend on unresolved near-zero margins.
[3263] CONJ-SIR7 — Incremental SIR recertification is local. Ordinary placement and obstacle changes reopen bounded dependency slices.
Falsification: routine changes repeatedly invalidate most of the project.
[3264] CONJ-SIR8 — Viewer projections can remain lightweight. Semantic IDs and derived meshes permit interactive visualization without duplicating the authoritative state.
Falsification: interactive workflows require hidden mutations not representable as SIR transactions.
[3265] CONJ-SIR9 — Route conflict cores are small. Many infeasibilities are explained by few obstacles, envelopes, or shared-capacity constraints.
Falsification: explanations remain globally diffuse.
[3266] CONJ-SIR10 — A hotel-scale PCSIR is workstation practical. A 190-room hotel admits a partitioned PCSIR on the target workstation.
Falsification: exact geometry, contextual interfaces, or proof dependencies exceed practical local storage and checking budgets.

[3350] 50. What has now been proven
[3351] Semantic identity
[3352] The selected SIR is a finite many-sorted relational pullback under explicit finiteness and compatibility assumptions.
[3353] Every integrated tuple is shared-identity consistent.
[3354] Nonempty component stores need not admit one integrated state.
[3355] Untyped graph structure can alias semantically distinct building states.
[3356] Display labels do not determine semantic identity.
[3357] Semantic identity does not determine artifact version.
[3358] Canonical serialization is deterministic under exact normalization rules.
[3359] Content identity remains distinct from physical correctness.
[3360] Type and unit correctness
[3361] Typed port composition preserves compatibility under checked interface isomorphism.
[3362] Dimension-incompatible composition is undefined without an adapter.
[3363] Trusted affine unit conversion preserves canonical physical value.
[3364] Equal numerical values in different units need not represent equal quantities.
[3365] Direction and orientation are part of quantity type.
[3366] Coordinate frames
[3367] Frame transforms compose along paths.
[3368] Zero holonomy establishes exact path independence.
[3369] A nonidentity exact cycle is an inconsistency certificate.
[3370] Sound interval cycle products support bounded inconsistency checking.
[3371] Identity inclusion in an interval product leaves consistency unresolved.
[3372] Exact and bounded geometry
[3373] Nearest-lattice quantization has an \(L^\infty\) error of at most \(\delta/2\).
[3374] Its three-dimensional Euclidean error is at most \(\sqrt3\delta/2\).
[3375] Strict geometric margin preserves separation under declared enclosure errors.
[3376] Zero-margin predicates can change under quantization.
[3377] Exact coordinate storage does not prove exact knowledge of the physical building.
[3378] Inner/outer geometry representations preserve bounded modality.
[3379] Spatial complexes
[3380] Valid oriented cell complexes satisfy \(\partial^2=0\).
[3381] Cell adjacency is distinct from penetrability and metric clearance.
[3382] Routing graphs are derived representations, not the spatial complex itself.
[3383] Graph soundness requires certified geometric embedding.
[3384] Ports and executable netlists
[3385] Ports carry component, medium, direction, frame, state, capacity, mode, and version semantics.
[3386] Port incompatibility empties a mandatory route-realization fiber.
[3387] Hypergraphs represent multi-terminal nets without arbitrary pairwise reduction.
[3388] Typed incidence supports explicit conservation under a declared sign convention.
[3389] Topological netlist validity does not imply geometric realization.
[3390] The same netlist under different placement may have different route fibers.
[3391] Identical rendered geometry may carry different netlists.
[3392] Route envelopes and configuration spaces
[3393] Fixed-orientation translational collision is exactly a Minkowski configuration-space obstacle.
[3394] A route through free configuration space has obstacle-free swept volume.
[3395] The same centerline may be feasible for one envelope and infeasible for another.
[3396] Endpoint connectivity does not imply geometric routability.
[3397] Centerline collision freedom does not imply bend feasibility.
[3398] Collision freedom does not imply maintenance accessibility.
[3399] Finite discretization can miss a continuous route.
[3400] Coarse relaxation can admit a spurious route.
[3401] Refining a derived approximation cannot invalidate a certificate checked against unchanged exact premises.
[3402] Homotopy and route realization
[3403] Endpoint-relative route classes belong naturally to a fundamental groupoid.
[3404] Topology rewrites may create, destroy, merge, or split route classes.
[3405] Route realization requires route, size, fittings, supports, penetrations, assemblies, modes, and proof.
[3406] Individual route fibers can all be nonempty while the joint fiber is empty.
[3407] Pairwise joint feasibility can hold while a triple is infeasible.
[3408] Pairwise separation does not establish shared rack or support capacity.
[3409] Modes and nonanticipativity
[3410] Scenario-wise or mode-wise feasible configurations need not form one nonanticipative reconfiguration policy.
[3411] Local-to-global structure
[3412] Running-intersection and separator agreement permit exact join-tree gluing.
[3413] Pairwise overlap agreement on a cyclic constraint system need not yield a global state.
[3414] Floor and shaft partitioning therefore requires explicit separator semantics.
[3415] Contextual interfaces
[3416] Router contextual equivalence is an equivalence relation.
[3417] Every admitted downstream computation factors through the canonical quotient.
[3418] The canonical quotient is the coarsest exact sufficient interface.
[3419] Experiment-family expansion refines or preserves the quotient.
[3420] A new objective can invalidate an old interface without changing route geometry.
[3421] A bounded interface cannot be silently consumed as exact.
[3422] Viewer projections
[3423] The viewer projection is generally noninjective.
[3424] A viewer is sufficient for a computation exactly when that computation factors through the viewer.
[3425] Visual correspondence does not prove semantic rewrite correspondence.
[3426] Rewrites and dependencies
[3427] Commuting exact transport preserves included semantics.
[3428] Incomplete correspondence requires set-valued transport or UNKNOWN.
[3429] Missing invalidation dependencies are unsound.
[3430] Dependency-directed SIR recertification equals cold checking under complete dependencies and identical checker semantics.
[3431] Unchanged local geometry does not alone permit route-certificate reuse.
[3432] Proposal-source replacement does not change checked certificate meaning.
[3433] Complexity and PCSIR
[3434] A locally shortest route can be absent from every globally preferred co-design.
[3435] Exact finite route realization contains the NP-hard graph Steiner Tree problem.
[3436] Explicit witness verification is linear in explicit representation and dependency size plus local checker costs.
[3437] PCSIR has a complete witness and release-fiber semantics.
[3438] Under THM-SIR63’s hypotheses, the PCSIR witness belongs to the SIR release fiber.
[3439] PCSIR validity remains open-world scoped.
