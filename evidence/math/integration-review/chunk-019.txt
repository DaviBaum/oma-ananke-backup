[20174] 84.16 Exact Scenario-Benders Example
[20175] First-stage variables:
[20176] \[ 0\le x_1,x_2\le5, \]
[20177] with first-stage cost:
[20178] \[ 2x_1+3x_2. \]
[20179] Three scenarios have probabilities:
[20180] \[ p_1=\frac12,\qquad p_2=\frac13,\qquad p_3=\frac16. \]
[20181] Recourse:
[20182] \[ Q_s(x)= \min_{y_s\ge0} q_sy_s \]
[20183] subject to:
[20184] \[ y_s\ge d_s-a_s^\top x. \]
[20185] Data:
[20186] \[ \begin{array}{c|c|c|c} s&q_s&d_s&a_s\\\hline 1&6&4&(1,1)\\ 2&4&3&(2,1)\\ 3&8&5&(1,2) \end{array} \]
[20188] \[ Q_s(x)=q_s(d_s-a_s^\top x)_+. \]
[20189] At \(x=(0,0)\), active duals are \(\pi_s=q_s\). The expected cut is:
[20190] \[ \theta \ge \frac12 6(4-x_1-x_2) + \frac13 4(3-2x_1-x_2) + \frac16 8(5-x_1-2x_2). \]
[20191] Collecting terms:
[20192] \[ \boxed{ \theta \ge \frac{68}{3} - 7x_1 - 7x_2. } \]
[20193] The master chooses the cheaper \(x_1\) direction:
[20194] \[ x_1=\frac{68}{21}, \qquad x_2=0, \qquad \theta=0. \]
[20195] At that point, scenario 2 has zero recourse, while scenarios 1 and 3 remain active. New duals are:
[20196] \[ (\pi_1,\pi_2,\pi_3)=(6,0,8). \]
[20197] The new cut is:
[20198] \[ \boxed{ \theta \ge \frac{56}{3} - \frac{13}{3}x_1 - \frac{17}{3}x_2. } \]
[20199] The master then selects:
[20200] \[ x_1=\frac{56}{13}, \qquad x_2=0. \]
[20201] Only scenario 3 remains active, yielding:
[20202] \[ \boxed{ \theta \ge \frac{20}{3} - \frac43x_1 - \frac83x_2. } \]
[20203] Solving the accumulated exact master gives:
[20204] \[ x_1=3,\qquad x_2=1,\qquad \theta=0, \]
[20205] with objective:
[20206] \[ 2(3)+3(1)=9. \]
[20207] All scenario deficits are nonpositive:
[20208] \[ \begin{aligned} 4-(3+1)&=0,\\ 3-(6+1)&=-4,\\ 5-(3+2)&=0. \end{aligned} \]
[20209] Direct piecewise-linear enumeration of the break regions confirms no lower objective exists, so the exact optimum is \(9\).
