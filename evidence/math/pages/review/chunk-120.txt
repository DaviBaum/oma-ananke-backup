[11-20:P24016; UTF16 799066] [ \sum_{i\in S}a_i=B. ]

[11-20:P24018; UTF16 799199] Both finish at time (B).
[11-20:P24019; UTF16 799224] Thus makespan (B) is feasible.
[11-20:P24020; UTF16 799255] If a schedule of makespan at most (B) exists
[11-20:P24021; UTF16 799300] At every time at most two activities overlap.
[11-20:P24022; UTF16 799346] Order activities by start time and greedily assign each interval to one of two resource slots.

[11-20:P24024; UTF16 799582] Therefore one of the two slots is free.

[11-20:P24026; UTF16 799685] Each class has total duration at most (B).
