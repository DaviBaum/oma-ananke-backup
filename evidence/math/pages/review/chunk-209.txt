[21-30:P33206; UTF16 1099641] THM-SOC10 — Weighted Proportional Fairness Is Equivalent to Weighted Log-Utility Optimality on a Convex Positive Feasible Set
[21-30:P33209; UTF16 1099785] \(\mathcal X\subseteq\mathbb R_{>0}^n\) is nonempty and convex;
[21-30:P33210; UTF16 1099849] wi>0.
[21-30:P33213; UTF16 1099864] L(x)=iwixi.
[21-30:P33214; UTF16 1099876] Then \(x^\star\in\mathcal X\) maximizes L iff:
[21-30:P33215; UTF16 1099923] \[ \sum_i w_i \frac{x_i-x_i^\star}{x_i^\star} \le0 \qquad \forall x\in\mathcal X. \]
[21-30:P33217; UTF16 1100026] Take arbitrary \(x\in\mathcal X\). Define:
[21-30:P33218; UTF16 1100069] \[ h(t) = L\left( x^\star+t(x-x^\star) \right), \qquad 0\le t\le1. \]
[21-30:P33219; UTF16 1100139] Convexity of \(\mathcal X\) keeps the segment feasible.
[21-30:P33220; UTF16 1100195] Because x maximizes L, t=0 maximizes h over this interval, so:
[21-30:P33221; UTF16 1100258] \[ h'(0^+)\le0. \]
[21-30:P33224; UTF16 1100283] h'(0+)=L(x)(x-x).
[21-30:P33227; UTF16 1100309] Lxi(x)=wixi,
[21-30:P33229; UTF16 1100333] \[ h'(0^+) = \sum_i w_i \frac{x_i-x_i^\star}{x_i^\star} \le0. \]
[21-30:P33230; UTF16 1100398] Thus x is proportionally fair.
[21-30:P33232; UTF16 1100447] Concavity of L gives:
[21-30:P33233; UTF16 1100469] \[ L(x) \le L(x^\star) + \nabla L(x^\star)\cdot(x-x^\star). \]

[21-30:P33235; UTF16 1100586] \[ \nabla L(x^\star)\cdot(x-x^\star)\le0. \]

[21-30:P33238; UTF16 1100667] for every \(x\in\mathcal X\).
[21-30:P33239; UTF16 1100697] Thus x maximizes L.
