# IFC-PORT-001: prior authored-port semantic checks superseded

Discovery date: 2026-09-14. This amendment applies to OMA-authored IFC route materializations that lack `port_axis_convention: IFC_FLOW_AXIS_V1`, including the first Digital Hub, Dental Clinic, Duplex, WBDG Office and WBDG Office simultaneous-route accepted exports. Original source assets, exported IFC bytes and prior versioned reports are retained unchanged.

The earlier exporter encoded every port Axis as its physical outward normal. IFC distribution ports instead encode an Axis that depends on FlowDirection: a SINK points into its owner and a SOURCE points out. Connected SOURCE/SINK flow axes align. The earlier exporter also omitted parent-relative port placement, and its optional existing-sink relationship was ordered in reverse. These defects affect the earlier port-semantic checks even where geometry, section, original STEP preservation and numerical clearance were checked. They do not retroactively establish physical collision or alter the recorded numerical distances. [Official IFC4 ADD2 TC1 definition](https://standards.buildingsmart.org/IFC/RELEASE/IFC4/ADD2_TC1/HTML/schema/ifcsharedbldgserviceelements/lexical/ifcdistributionport.htm), [official IFC2X3 CV2.0 implementation agreement](https://standards.buildingsmart.org/documents/Implementation/IFC_Implementation_Agreements/CV-2x3-176.html).

The coordinated correction emits flow-dependent axes and owner-relative placements, uses fixed `IfcRelNests` ownership for IFC4 and `IfcRelConnectsPortToElement` for IFC2X3, and orders every connection SOURCE to SINK. A fresh checker independently reconstructs physical outward normals from the actual encoded axes and checks them against native body caps. Ownership enumeration retains missing, duplicate and ambiguous relationships; it cannot silently select one owner. Existing terminal binding checks the immutable original port identity, direction, position, orientation, connection occupancy and supported physical section. Unknown section or ownership remains a failure.

The new manifest marker is an informational version marker, not a certificate. Adversarial tests add the marker to old or malformed native bytes and confirm that the fresh reimport, semantic or native cap check rejects them. Current acceptance/export gates also reject known affected historical materializations. Prior recorded PASS results remain historical evidence under their exact previous build; their port-semantic conclusion is superseded by this amendment.

Regeneration creates a new project or candidate from each original source import and immutable run mission, materializes new IFC bytes with the corrected writer, runs the current independent checker, accepts only a current-build result and freshly checks the exported replacement. Accepted historical models are never edited in place. For a federation, all original source objects and all route pairs remain in the checker denominator. A reference geometry check performed under the old port convention remains only a scoped geometry feasibility observation until corrected ports are regenerated and fully checked.

Binding to an existing source terminal does not grant an exemption for its owner. The current all-source obstacle checker still blocks or rejects physical owner contact unless a supported local interface authorization has independently established its exact scope. This correction does not introduce a blanket same-system or neighboring-element clearance exemption.
