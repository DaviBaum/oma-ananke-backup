# Current checked incumbent selection

Single-route, joint-route and shared-network optimization distinguish physical
evidence from finite arithmetic. Before admission, a candidate must still belong
to the exact run, project and physical kind, have a complete current executable
PASS bound to its state/mission/rules, preserve the declared objective weights,
and include exactly the complete mandatory physical check inventory and its
scenario-dependent PASS/NOT_APPLICABLE states, without duplicate check IDs.
Every original IFC source, materialized replacement and preserved prior joint
route artifact is hash-checked. The prior native/numerical physical report is
reused only under those matching applicability conditions; CAD is not rerun by
the selection adapter.

The arithmetic rows are refreshed after candidate generation. A concurrent
independent recheck can invalidate an earlier result while later alternatives
are being examined; that old `CHECKED` flag and cost do not remain eligible.
Final selection reads current evidence again and revalidates the selected report
pointer and bytes before recording its disposition. Accepted revisions still use
the separate optimistic-concurrency and independent-report gates.

Reported length and fitting-count values are scalarized with exact rational
arithmetic using the declared decimal values and weights. Joint selection sums
those exact weighted per-route values. Network master fingerprints now include
the serialized objective policy. The native lengths themselves retain their
numerical geometry model; exact scalarization does not certify exact lengths.

If the finite solver stops before returning an incumbent, a complete separately
checked physical candidate can still be retained. Its
`oma.current-checked-incumbent/1` artifact records the current report/root/version,
objective policy and cost, exclusions, and the interrupted arithmetic selection.
It is explicitly a feasible incumbent, with no master optimum, continuous/global
optimum, global lower bound or global gap claim. No current applicable candidate
produces `NO_CURRENT_CHECKED_INCUMBENT`, never an invented physical infeasibility.

Regressions exercise genuine native rechecks that turn a cached PASS into FAIL,
finite solvers stopped after one node, tampered report applicability, and changed
physical bytes. The native checker mathematics and fabrication are unchanged.
