# Hospital alignment and installed-service audit

The seven IFC files do not establish an approved common engineering frame through the inspected datums and identities. This audit does not approve an identity transform, remove a discipline, change an IFC, or invent installed service demands. The preserved full-federation `MISSING_INPUTS` and separate ARC-only native timeout remain governing results.

All seven site placement matrices contain the same approximately -5 degree rotation. ARC, STR and SPR have site Z=165.8112 m; ELE, FIRE, MECH and PLB have Z=165.8377988178 m, a 26.5988178 mm difference. Similar placements alone cannot decide whether that difference is intentional or an export offset. TrueNorth disagrees by approximately 5 degrees between groups. Site geospatial declarations disagree too. There is no IfcMapConversion, IfcProjectedCRS or IfcGrid in these retained raw-datum records, and the six non-ARC Site/Building/Project identities differ from ARC.

Named storeys provide clues, not a sufficient common datum. MECH Level 7 is about 31 m above its base, matching ARC Level 7A rather than ARC Level 7 at 34 m. SPR levels 2 through 5 differ from ARC by +0.096, -0.0272, -0.1504 and -0.2736 m. No single vertical translation makes those elevations agree. STR TOS names distinguish a 0.15 m structural offset. Treating every similarly named level as the same physical plane would discard those distinctions. A complete scan found no unique exact physical type-and-name pair shared between ARC and any other discipline. This is a bounded semantic probe; it does not claim that an expensive geometric correspondence search could find no recognizable common objects.

The exact input needed to approve federation is a source-hash-bound engineering frame declaration: one reference frame and unit convention, a rigid source-world-metre-to-reference transform for each discipline, residual/tolerance bounds, and provenance identifying who or what establishes the correspondence. An author-provided coordination transform is preferable. Alternatively, explicitly identified corresponding physical control points (at least three non-collinear points per rigid 3-D mapping, with redundant independent controls), their coordinate frames and uncertainty, plus a reviewed interpretation of the level and TrueNorth discrepancies, can support an independently checked transform. The Site coordinates and generic names here do not supply that missing identity.

The complete imported inventory has 85,602 ports and 42,801 explicit port connections. Every port occurs in exactly one connection; there are zero unconnected ports and zero extra connection endpoints outside the port inventory. PLB includes 582 building-element proxies, including recognizable toilets; SPR includes 1,354 fire suppression terminals. MECH includes named pumps and boilers. Installed asset examples with source hashes, STEP IDs, GUIDs, placements and port identities are retained separately. These are existing serviced assets, not free attachment points.

No imported product property-set key contains `flow` or `pressure` in this audit. Names such as `Pump - Base Mounted:38 LPS - 358 kPa Head` are descriptive strings, not a verified operating point, pump curve, available total-pressure boundary or minimum terminal delivery. Systems include domestic cold/hot water, sanitary, fire protection and mechanical services; those laws must not be silently interchanged. The audit does not assert that every potentially relevant free-text or external engineering document is absent.

A meaningful installed-service change therefore needs a chosen service and exact source/sink asset and port identities, the intended operation (new independent service versus an authorized modification to an existing connected network), preservation obligations for existing connections, and a numerical service contract. That contract must state delivery minima or fixed flows and available pressure convention, fluid and loss assumptions, admissible sizes/velocities, permitted regions and complete obstacle scope. Adding another connection to an already occupied port or splitting an original pipe is not authorized by the current hypothetical request. The additive-only generated-tree workflow should not pretend it performed that retrofit.

The immediate honest path is to finish the already declared ARC-only hypothetical mission with unchanged ARC obstacles, delivery assumptions and computational limits, using only sound geometry acceleration. It can yield a meaningful new-service routing result against that architectural source, but cannot become a claim about existing Hospital service or full seven-discipline coordination. The separate seven-source mission stays blocked until engineering alignment is established. Its original obstacles must remain present when it is resumed.

`audit.py` reads the seven preserved complete source-audit inventories and raw-datum records, and freshly hashes all original IFC bytes before/after. It performs no CAD extraction or Store writes. `result.json` records exact inputs and findings; `installed-asset-examples.json` retains bounded identity-bearing examples. The original source count and hash denominator is complete.
